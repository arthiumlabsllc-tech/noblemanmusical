# Admin Panel

## Overview

Full management interface for Nobleman Musical Center operations.

## Pages

- `/admin` — Dashboard with KPIs
- `/admin/products` — Product management (CRUD, bulk actions)
- `/admin/categories` — Category management
- `/admin/brands` — Brand management
- `/admin/orders` — Order management
- `/admin/customers` — Customer list
- `/admin/quotes` — B2B quote inbox
- `/admin/inventory` — Stock management
- `/admin/discounts` — Discount codes
- `/admin/workers` — Worker management
- `/admin/roles` — Role and permission management
- `/admin/reports` — Sales and POS reports
- `/admin/settings` — Store settings

## Dashboard KPIs

- Today's revenue
- Today's orders
- Average order value
- POS sales today
- Pending quotes
- Low stock alerts
